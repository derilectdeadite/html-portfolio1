The Best Games According to Bryan

My top 5 games of all-time, ranked in order from highest to lowest.

Bloodborne

This is my favourite game. I love the dark atmosphere and challenging combat. The 5 D's apply. I just wish it would be ported to PC

VrChat

A VR game that allows interaction between users through beautiful worlds and wacky avatars.

WarThunder

The game may be buggy but hey, its addicting.

Escape from Tarkov

This realistic FPS is a challenge, and the mechanics can take time to master, but as a player its a very interesting kind of grind.

Valheim

Who DOESN'T want to be a viking running around and building till your heart's content?
